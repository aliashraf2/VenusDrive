Scope
57 brands from the prior filtered list; only years present in the supplied Pasted text.txt.

Sources
NHTSA vPIC API: https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/api/ ; EPA/DOE FuelEconomy.gov CSV: https://www.fueleconomy.gov/feg/epadata/vehicles.csv.zip

Collected
2026-10-25

Completeness
Partial catalog. Not a complete RockAuto export or complete history of US vehicle sales.

Older years
NHTSA year-specific endpoint documents ModelYear greater than 1995. EPA/DOE records supplement older years from 1984 and make/year gaps. Pre-1984 years remain uncollected.

Vehicle types
NHTSA: included model IDs from Passenger Car, Multipurpose Passenger Vehicle (MPV), or Truck lookups. EPA: passenger/light-duty vehicle catalog classes. Motorcycles and ATVs excluded.

Vehicle type limitation
Type membership is collected across all years for the make; it is not a verified body classification for each model-year.

Names and IDs
Source model names retained and may differ from RockAuto. NHTSA model IDs shown where available; EPA model groups have blank model IDs. EPA labels may include drivetrain/body variants. Engines and part fitment not included.

Source precedence
NHTSA used for 1996 onward when eligible models were returned. EPA used only where that make/year has no NHTSA models or is older than 1996. Sources were not unioned within a make/year, so additional models may remain missing.

Empty results
A zero means no eligible models were collected, not proof that no vehicles were produced or sold that year.

2027
Future model-year records are included only where supplied years and NHTSA responses support them; coverage may be incomplete.

Validation
Exact make-name matching, eligible vehicle-type filtering, duplicate model-ID checks per make/year, and reconciliation of detail and coverage counts.

Fitment
This is a make/year/model reference, not a parts compatibility or maintenance schedule database.

Screenshot
Used as a layout reference only. Screenshot-specific model names were not merged into NHTSA data.
